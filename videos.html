CNA Street

“CNA Street - a city street built with CNA” (22 September 2026, 2 min 39 s): a procedurally generated European street with traffic, pedestrians, shadows and reflections. It is playable in the browser on the Demos page.

Wolf CNA

“Wolf CNA” (19 September 2026, 1 min): a retro first-person shooter prototype with polygonal 3D rendering, playable in the browser on the Demos page.

CNA Car Simulator #2

“CNA Car Simulator #2 - Helicopter was added” (19 September 2026, 4 min 16 s): a follow-up showing the new helicopter.

CNA Car Simulator

“CNA Car Simulator” (15 September 2026, 6 min 8 s): a passenger-car driving simulator in a fictional Czech landscape built on CNA.

Living Room Simulator

“Living Room Simulator - a CNA 3D demo” (14 September 2026, 3 min 35 s): an explorable 3D living room.

Demos

Demo Videos

CNA running real game code and demo applications.

House 3D Demo

“CNA (reimplementation of XNA 4.0) now partially supports 3D” — the first basic 3D support, recorded on 3 May 2026 (48 seconds, Open Eggbert channel). For a playable 3D example, try the House 3D Demo on the Demos page.

Speedy Blupi on CNA

“Speedy Blupi for Windows Phone was ported to C++” — the 2013 game running on Linux, recorded on 25 April 2026 (1 min 41 s, Open Eggbert channel). The port is built on CNA (see Speedy Blupi on the Demos page); the video itself does not name CNA.

CNA 2D Demo

“CNA (C++ reimplementation of XNA 4.0) now supports 2D” — the first 2D support built on SDL 3, recorded on 11 April 2026 (13 seconds, Open Eggbert channel), showing thousands of sprites drawn at once.
