Historical

The CNA Bible, alpha.1 edition

⚠

This is a historical document. The PDF below describes CNA v0.1.0-alpha.1 (commit 1bb2145d, 20 August 2026). It is not maintained and it does not describe the current snapshot, 009d40f5: among other things its renderer inventory (50 identities), its C API version (ABI 0.7.0) and its lists of known limitations are out of date.

The CNA Bible was a book-length technical manual for CNA. Its online editions (bible.libcna.com and book.libcna.com) are being retired. This site keeps the last sealed PDF for reference only.

Where the current knowledge lives

The book's technical content was re-checked against snapshot 009d40f5, corrected where CNA had changed, and absorbed into the pages of this site. Use these instead of the PDF:

  • Deep Dives — the long-form explanations: exact semantics, invariants, evidence and limits, by subject.
  • Known Issues — the current bugs, functional gaps, platform limitations and verification gaps; defects the book recorded that have since been fixed are not listed.
  • Development — the maintainer layer: architecture, internals, handbook and generated references.
  • Documentation and Tutorials — the user-facing guides.
