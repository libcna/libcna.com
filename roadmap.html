Roadmap

Development Roadmap

CNA is a long-term project developed incrementally. This page describes the honest current state and the path forward - no overpromising, no fake ETAs.

⚠

Honest timeline note: CNA is a long-term open-source project maintained in spare time. There are no fixed release dates. Milestones are goals, not commitments. Progress is incremental and driven by API coverage, renderer stability, and real-world testing against ported games.

Current State

Where CNA actually is

roughly 898,000 lines of C++ across about 2,900 source and header files, and nearly 4,900 commits. CNA implements 357 public types across the Microsoft.Xna.Framework namespaces — every XNA 4.0 namespace except Framework.Design. Rather than a blended coverage percentage, CNA pins its public surface with compile-time signature-freeze tests and a CNA_STRICT_XNA_API purity mode. Verification stands at 6,818 GoogleTest case definitions across 433 test files, 1,621 registered renderer pixel/smoke tests, and a 39-scene XNA oracle corpus captured from the real XNA 4.0 runtime, which the DIRECTX9 renderer matches exactly at zero tolerance.

Read that as: breadth is largely done, depth is not. The sections below separate what has shipped from what is genuinely partial and what nobody has started.

Shipped

What is done and verified

Everything in this list exists in the source tree today and was confirmed by audit rather than by changelog.

Complete

46 renderers, selectable at build time Shipped

SDL_RENDERER, OPENGLES3, BGFX, VULKAN, WEBGPU, HEADLESS, SOFTWARE, DIRECTX11, DIRECTX12, CANVAS, FREEDIRECT, DIRECTX9 and SDL_GPU, chosen with -DCNA_GRAPHICS_RENDERER. Exactly one per binary; there is no runtime dispatch and no fallback. The shared interface, IGraphicsRenderer, has 132 virtuals of which 59 are pure. Maturity tracks age: EasyGL, Vulkan, bgfx and SDL_Renderer date from April 2026, and the other nine were created within roughly the last two weeks.

Production-ready

EasyGL renderer — the most mature path Shipped

5,305 LOC and 239 CTests. 13 GLSL programs, all 9 effect families, per-vertex and per-pixel lighting, instancing, occlusion queries, anisotropy, fog, and GL context-loss recovery. Only 4 virtuals go unoverridden. This is the Linux default and the renderer everything else is measured against.

Complete — was listed here as "planned, not started"

Direct3D 9 renderer — functional, and the pixel-exact XNA oracle target Shipped

This page previously described DIRECTX9 as a possible future renderer that had not been authorized. That is out of date: DIRECTX9 is 6,902 LOC with 19 CTests, Windows-only, with 43 of its 45 overrides real, 72 verified SM2/SM3 shader blobs, full device-lost and D3DPOOL_DEFAULT recovery, and caps-validated MRT. It is the one renderer held to pixel-exact XNA 4.0 authenticity rather than a "looks right" bar, and it is the renderer the 39-scene oracle corpus is diffed against at --tolerance 0.

Complete

Vulkan renderer Shipped

10,506 LOC and 138 CTests: 35 SPIR-V entry points, 8 effect paths, an MRT proxy, device-queried depth formats, a real stencil PSO, MSAA resolve and mip blit cascades. Functional, close to production.

New

SDL_GPU renderer Shipped

6,683 LOC and 21 CTests. A deferred/retained design with 23 SPIR-V blobs, 9 effect families, stencil and MSAA resolve. Its device layer is Vulkan-only today.

New

FreeDirect renderer — the best 2D fidelity in the project Shipped

1,178 LOC and 9 CTests, 2D only. DirectDraw-shaped, fronting the sibling free-direct project: a real CPU compositor, bilinear filtering, 4 distinct blend formulas, and real Wrap/Mirror addressing — a genuine win over SDL_Renderer. Cross-platform despite the name.

New

ASCII effect Migrated

The former ASCII renderer was deliberately removed and its logic promoted to a CNAEXT post-process effect, CNA::Graphics::AsciiPostProcessEffect. Because it now works through the public Texture2D::GetData() and SpriteBatch API, it applies on any renderer and to 3D-rendered sources — which the old renderer could not do. Requires -DCNA_CNAEXT=ON.

New — but untested

Canvas renderer (Emscripten) Shipped, untested

1,177 LOC: 11 EM_JS functions with careful un-premultiply and clip handling, mature in design and narrow in scope. The caveat is stated rather than buried: it has zero CTests, and no automated test has ever rendered a single Canvas pixel.

Complete

The .xnb content pipeline Shipped

A real XNB reader, roughly 4,000 LOC, wired into ContentManager: 50 registered type readers covering primitives, math types, textures, SpriteFont, SoundEffect, Song, the stock effects and the model family, on top of a real 31 KB LZX decompressor, the full FNA ContentReader surface, and two-pass shared-resource resolution, fuzz-hardened against malformed files. 24 test files and 134 test cases run against binary MonoGame fixtures. ContentManager prefers a .xnb when one exists and falls back to loose files otherwise. Note that you must call RegisterAllBuiltInXnbReaders() once at startup — a deliberate design decision, but it means the registry is empty by default.

Complete

PBR effects and skeletal animation Shipped

Beyond the six XNA stock effects (all implemented natively in C++, not translated from bytecode), CNA ships non-XNA PbrEffect and SkinnedPbrEffect, plus RenderPipelineSettings, PbrMaterial, TonemappingMode and ShadowQuality. Skeletal animation is real: SkinnedModelEXT with AnimationPlayer, AnimationClipEXT, BoneTrackEXT and KeyframeEXT, plus MorphTargetEXT blend shapes. All of it sits behind the CNAEXT boundary, so it is visible at compile time as non-XNA surface.

Complete

Pixel-exact verification against the real XNA runtime Shipped

39 scenes under tools/xna-oracle/, rendered by a real C#/XNA reference renderer and diffed byte-for-byte against CNA's DIRECTX9 output at --tolerance 0. Alongside it: differential testing against a real running FNA build (tools/fna-reference/), and a compile-time CNAEXT purity check that turns all 1,289 non-XNA declarations into [[deprecated]] warnings under -Werror.

Complete

A real Input, Audio, Net and Storage core Shipped

Input has zero stubs and a SDL3 GamePad bridge. XACT is a real parser and player, not a facade — a real binary parser for the XGS/XWB/XSB formats with FACT's actual volume formula. Net is real ENet over genuine UDP with real LAN discovery. Storage is real std::filesystem IO with correct player namespacing. See XNA Compatibility for the per-namespace breakdown.

In Progress

Partial, and known to be partial

These exist and work in part. The gaps below are specific and audited, not vague.

Partial

DIRECTX12 — missing scissor, viewport, stencil and blend factor Partial

6,819 LOC with a strong core: PSO and root-signature caches and a barrier tracker. But there is no scissor, no viewport, no stencil and no blend factor, and MRT clears without ever drawing. One CTest covers the entire renderer.

Partial

WebGPU — no render targets at all Partial

7,110 LOC and 14 CTests, and today a backbuffer-only forward renderer: no render targets, no MSAA, no cube or 3D textures, no queries, no instancing, and a non-functional stencil path. Render-target support is the gating item.

Partial

bgfx — no D3D or Metal shader blobs Partial

4,367 LOC and 114 CTests, with 108 shader blobs built for GL, GLES, Vulkan and WebGPU only. The consequence is worth stating plainly: on bgfx's default Windows and macOS renderers, 3D silently draws nothing. Separately, CreateIndexBuffer32 is never overridden, so DrawUserIndexedPrimitives uploads 32-bit indices into a 16-bit buffer and corrupts geometry.

Thin

Continuous integration Partial

Three workflows exist and only one, input-ci.yml, is auto-triggered — ubuntu-24.04, gcc-14, a 5-way renderer matrix, running only the input-labelled tests. devices-tests.yml is path-filtered; d3d-windows-ci.yml is manual dispatch only. There is no macOS, Android or Emscripten CI, and the full CnaTests suite cannot build on any Windows toolchain because roughly 10 files use POSIX ::setenv(). Because renderer choice is compile-time, full coverage would need 14 separate configure, build and test runs.

Open

Truthful capability reporting Partial

The base implementation returns true for every capability except multi-stream vertex input, and DIRECTX9, DIRECTX11, DIRECTX12 and SDL_GPU never override SupportsCapability() at all — so they advertise MSAA, MRT, occlusion queries, custom effects and instancing without ever querying the device. Several other renderers advertise features whose implementation throws or silently does nothing. Because the default fails open, a true means "not contradicted", not "verified".

Not Started

Real gaps nobody has begun

Highest impact

Compiled .fx shader bytecode Not started

Effect(GraphicsDevice&, byte[]) throws NotImplementedException unconditionally, and there is no MojoShader equivalent anywhere in the tree. Games with custom HLSL effects cannot run; custom shaders are reachable only by hand-writing GLSL or SPIR-V for the CNAEXT ShaderEffect, per renderer. This is the single biggest real porting gap in the project.

Planned

Surface formats beyond SurfaceFormat::Color Not started

Texture::ValidateFormat throws for all 25 other formats, and it is called from both Texture2D constructors and from Texture3D and TextureCube. DXT and DDS files therefore load only by CPU-decompressing to RGBA, and float, half and HDR render targets are unavailable despite the enum listing them.

Planned

ReflectiveReader for custom XNB types Not started

The XNB pipeline covers the stock types well and cannot load a single user-defined one. C++ has no reflection, so this needs a design decision before any code. Missing alongside it: EnumReader, ExternalReferenceReader, VideoReader and a real EffectReader, which is currently an always-throwing placeholder.

Planned

A GamerServices service renderer Not started

All 52 types are present with complete member signatures. There is no online service behind them — no Steam, no Xbox Live, no HTTP — but CNA does not stop at FNA's no-op shim: achievements and leaderboard entries are genuinely persisted as JSON under SDL_GetPrefPath() and reload in a later process, and LeaderboardWriter/LeaderboardReader implement real sorting, ranking and paging. Guide's message-box and keyboard-input paths are real implementations with overlay rendering. What genuinely does nothing is the remaining fifteen Guide::Show* overlay entry points, and the XNA-faithful AvatarRenderer::Draw() — the working avatar path is the CNAEXT DrawRealEXT().

Planned

The Media catalogue layer Not started

14 types — MediaLibrary, MediaSource, Album, Artist, Genre, Playlist, Picture, PictureAlbum and their 6 collections — throw on every member, and MediaLibrary::SavePicture(string, Stream*) is declared with no definition anywhere, which makes it a link error rather than a throw. Playback itself is real: MediaPlayer through SDL3_mixer and VideoPlayer through FFmpeg, though FFmpeg is unavailable on Emscripten, Android and every Windows target.

Planned

Tests for Media, Storage and Canvas Not started

Media and Storage have zero unit tests, and the Canvas renderer has zero CTests. Storage in particular is believed complete and correct, which is exactly the situation where an absent suite is worth flagging.

Long term

Public stable release (v1.0) Long term

A versioned release other projects can safely depend on. Reaching it plausibly requires .fx support (or an explicit decision never to have it), surface formats beyond Color, coverage for the untested namespaces, CI that gates the full unit suite on at least one platform, and an API stability guarantee — which is not something to offer at the current rate of change.

🚀

Contributing: CNA is open source (Ms-PL). The highest-value contributions right now are the unglamorous ones — writing the missing Media and Storage tests and the first Canvas pixel test, closing the named renderer gaps, making SupportsCapability() truthful, and removing the POSIX ::setenv() dependency so the test suite can build on Windows. See the GitHub repository for open issues and the TODO list, and the detailed roadmap for the full breakdown.
