CNA-PLAT-016: On Apple GPUs SamplerState.MipMapLevelOfDetailBias is refused by METAL below macOS/iOS 26, in builds against an older SDK and on devices that ignore it, and does not reach the GPU on FNA3D's Metal-backed driver

METAL throws NotSupportedException for a non-zero sampler LOD bias unless the OS and the build SDK are 26 or later and the device honours the bias; FNA3D on Apple hands the bias to SDL's Metal backend, which ignores it.

Affected contract
SamplerState.MipMapLevelOfDetailBias on the METAL renderer, and on the FNA3D renderer on macOS and iOS

Expected behaviour

XNA 4.0 applies SamplerState.MipMapLevelOfDetailBias on every device: a bias of +1 selects the next smaller mip level, and a game that sharpens or blurs textures with it gets the same mip selection everywhere.

Actual behaviour at TARGET

  • METAL: samplerFor in MetalRenderer.mm maps the bias onto MTLSamplerDescriptor.lodBias, which exists only from macOS/iOS 26 and is compiled only when the build SDK declares it (CNA_METAL_SDK_HAS_SAMPLER_LOD_BIAS, AM4-099). A non-zero bias on an older OS, or from a build made with an older SDK, throws System::NotSupportedException (‘needs macOS/iOS 26 and a build against their SDK’). Even then, the first non-zero bias runs a one-time probe (metalDeviceHonoursSamplerLodBias, AM4-296): CNA measured that the Apple Paravirtual device of the hosted macOS runners accepts lodBias and ignores it, so on a device that ignores it the bias is refused the same way. A bias of 0 always works.
  • FNA3D on Apple: FNA3D selects its SDL_GPU driver, which runs on SDL's Metal backend; that backend ignores mip_lod_bias, and FNA3D's MojoShader shaders have no other route for it, so the bias is accepted and has no effect. Fna3dRenderer::SamplerLodBiasReachesTheGpuEXT() (Fna3dRenderer.cpp, AM4-191) reports false on Apple for every driver but OpenGL; nothing is thrown.

CNA's own SDL_GPU renderer is not affected for stock effects, which carry the bias in their shaders (SDLGPU-121).

Evidence

Read at c1c316b9: the Metal sampler cache and its probe, FNA3D's SamplerLodBiasReachesTheGpuEXT, the shared mip contract test and CNA's records; nothing was executed here. The behaviour on devices is CNA's own measurement: AM4-296 records a CNA-free probe on the hosted runner's Apple Paravirtual device that samples level 0 at a +1 and a +2 sampler bias while the shader's own bias selects levels 1 and 2, and a Mac mini M4 that honours it; AM4-191 records that FNA3D's SDL_GPU driver on Metal ignores the bias. The pre-26 refusal follows from the @available guard and the SDK macro; CNA notes it was not executed against a real pre-26 SDK. Related but older and outside Apple: MipMapLevelOfDetailBias has no OpenGL ES equivalent, so EasyGL's OPENGLES3 and WEBGL2 identities accept it without effect, as fx-compiled-effects.md documents.

Focused reproduction

No focused reproduction is known. Nothing has been invented here; the evidence above is what exists.

Current tests

texture_filter_mip_contract_test.cpp draws one raw biased draw first and accepts a NotSupportedException on Metal only (any other renderer refusing fails); the FNA3D compiled-effect sampler contract passes SamplerLodBiasReachesTheGpuEXT() as its supportsLodBias option, so on Apple it skips the bias section. No test asserts the pre-26 refusal on a real older OS or SDK.

Regression test

The existing contract tests are the regression tests; what is missing is a run on macOS below 26 (or a build against an older SDK) that confirms the refusal, and, if FNA3D gains a shader route for the bias, removing the Apple exception from its sampler contract.

Blast radius

Games that set a non-zero MipMapLevelOfDetailBias: on METAL they get an exception on macOS/iOS below 26, from a build made with an older Xcode SDK, or on a GPU that ignores the bias (the hosted macOS CI runners); on FNA3D on Apple the textures are sampled as if the bias were 0. A bias of 0, every other sampler state, and the other renderers on Apple are unaffected.

Workaround

Keep the bias at 0 on Apple, or catch the exception and fall back; alternatively bias the mip level in the shader of a compiled effect, which on the device CNA measured is honoured where the sampler's bias is ignored. Build METAL with an Xcode that ships the macOS/iOS 26 SDK when the sampler bias is needed.
