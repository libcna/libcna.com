Gate C's retake matrix (Fox, DamagedHelmet, Sponza and the sparse cases against the Khronos sample renderer) is recorded passing on OPENGLES3; the two other renderers it passed on were retired, and the row needs four including a Direct3D path.

Affected contract
glTF import and rendering compared with the Khronos glTF-Sample-Renderer on real assets

Expected behaviour

plan_gltf.md §27.2 row 12 asks for the retake matrix to pass on at least four renderers, including one Direct3D path.

Actual behaviour at TARGET

plan_gltf.md §27.2 row 12 is open and was restated on 2026-10-13: two of the three renderers Gate C had passed on (all 14 rows, 15 cases, against each renderer's own goldens) were retired, and the harness lost its Wine route with them, so the row now needs surviving renderers (the plan names SOFTWARE, SDL_GPU and WEBGPU as candidates), and its Direct3D clause can only be met by DIRECTX9 under Wine. The remaining pass is OPENGLES3's. A further run is blocked by the harness's two external inputs (a built Khronos glTF-Sample-Renderer and a sparse glTF-Sample-Assets checkout), which the plan records as absent on its host. gltf-viewer-retake-report.json records only the OPENGLES3 run (2026-08-14, CNA commit d3c5bca6).

Source locations

  • plans/plan_gltf.md — section 27.2 row 12 (restated 2026-10-13) and GLTF-459
  • docs/gltf-viewer-retake-report.json — the recorded Gate C run and its pins
