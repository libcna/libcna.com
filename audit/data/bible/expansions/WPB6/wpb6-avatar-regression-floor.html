The avatar visual review

The renderer comparison above comes from tools/avatar_builder/avatar_review.py, which renders review sheets through the real renderer (cna_avatar_review) and through a CPU preview. It is an image review, not a gate: no CTest registration or workflow runs it, and the figures quoted are CNA's own record from 2026-09-30, not re-measured for this page.

An older structural check, {{src:scripts/avatar_visual_regression_check.py|avatar_visual_regression_check.py}}, is still in the tree. It runs cna_demo_avatar under xvfb-run for three pinned views and counts “speckle” (dark pixels touching bright ones) in fixed pixel boxes, against ceilings recorded on 2026-07-18. Those boxes and ceilings describe the avatar the demo drew then; the demo now draws a random standard avatar from the current catalog, so the script's floor is not a measure of today's avatars. Its own header says that passing does not mean the avatar looks correct. Read at c1c316b9; not executed.
